What's new in 0.3.0

- Automatic port proxying, so remote services open in the app.
- Live port forwarding controls for the current session.
- Multi-client MonkeyMux sessions that follow the client you use.
- Android device debugging over SSH with wireless ADB pairing.
- Animated inline terminal graphics (GIF and APNG).
- Cancel a stuck connection attempt.
- In-app browser uploads, camera, and site permissions.
- Themed home-screen shortcut icons.
- Keyboard, IME, and MonkeyMux fixes.
