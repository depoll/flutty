What's new in 0.3.0

- Automatic port proxying: remote services are discovered while you work and forwarded over SSH, so localhost links in the terminal open straight into the in-app browser.
- Live port forwarding controls: start, stop, add, and edit tunnels for the current session from the terminal without reconnecting.
- Multi-client MonkeyMux sessions: attach from your phone and your desktop at the same time, with the shared window following whichever client you are using and tmux-style Ctrl-B window controls.
- Animated terminal graphics: inline GIF and APNG playback with Kitty animation support, paused when Reduce Motion is on.
- Home-screen shortcuts now use per-host themed icons.
- MonkeyMux restores your session automatically after helper upgrades, with steadier theme changes, resizes, and image pastes.
- On-screen keyboard and IME fixes, tappable Copilot CLI links, and a long list of terminal and stability improvements.
